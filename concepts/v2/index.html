Personal site · round two · Nov 2026

Three sites you step into, not scroll past.

Each one is a working prototype that opens full screen. Use a laptop for the first look. Projects and numbers are placeholders, and you can drop your own photo into any of them.

  1. 01

    Gargantua

    Cinema · Interstellar, Kip Thorne’s physics

    Your name, bent by a black hole.A black hole ray-traced in real time, lensing the type behind it. Hover a project and gravity bends its name instead. Click and you fall through the event horizon into the project. A clock counts Earth years by Miller’s planet time.

    Tesseract

    Book culture · Murph’s bookshelf

    Your work as an endless library behind the shelf.An infinite lattice of shelves seen from behind. Every project is a book, and every shelf is a year. Scroll to move through time. Pull a book out and it opens into a printed spread. Your portrait sits on the About the Author page, and every so often the dust falls in Morse code.

    Architecture · Tadao Ando, TARS

    Four slabs in raking light.The restrained one. Concrete monoliths that pivot and walk like TARS to reveal each section. Your portrait is carved into the stone as a relief that appears as the light moves. Honesty and humor dials rewrite your bio.
